ImpedanceMimic Learned Impedance as Action Space for Force-Accurate Motion Imitation

Anonymous Authors

Video Paperunder review Code Checkpointssoon

ImpedanceMimic is a reinforcement-learning policy for physics-based motion imitation that modulates the stiffness, damping and position setpoint of every joint. Trained on the motion and force-plate recordings of 129 people across six activities, it reproduces the movement and the contact forces together, and needs only motion at inference. The same policy transfers without retraining to unseen people, to activities withheld from training and to a simulated humanoid robot, and its gains encode motor state: stiffer under load, soft before the foot leaves the ground.

Gains that encode motor state

The policy is free to set stiffness and damping at every joint and every instant. What it learns follows the state of the movement: stiffer under load, and softer before the foot leaves the ground.
